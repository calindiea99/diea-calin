Jekyll is a simple, blog-aware, static site generator perfect for personal, project, or organization sites. Some key features include:

- **Markdown Support**: Write posts in Markdown
- **Liquid Templating**: Powerful templating engine
- **GitHub Pages Ready**: Easy deployment to GitHub Pages
